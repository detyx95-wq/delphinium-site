Delphinium Interactive builds bespoke 3D configurators for jewellery and luxury houses. No plugin, no template: each configurator is designed to honour the material, showcase the variants and guide the buyer to a decision, with the precision of a goldsmith's workshop.

Our method

  1. First, we listen.

    An audit of your catalogue, your conversion challenges and your technical constraints. Together we set the return on investment to aim for.

  2. Then we model.

    High-fidelity models from your HD photos, 3D files or CAD. Materials, lighting and interface are validated with you on a prototype.

  3. We deploy in real time.

    A 3D engine optimised for the web: fast loading, mobile and augmented reality, careful SEO.

  4. You sell in three weeks.

    Integration with your shop, testing and tracking of first results, then dedicated maintenance. About two working weeks for the Pro offer, three for Premium.
